- 🚗 Multi-vehicle garage and default vehicle management
- 🅿️ Parking search with area, slot type and availability filters

- 📱 Secure QR pass generation and verification
- 🧾 Downloadable digital PDF receipts
- 🔔 Notifications and complete payment history
- 👨‍💼 Admin parking-area and slot management
- ✅ Check-in/check-out and payment ledger
- 📈 Reports, occupancy and revenue monitoring

### ♻️ 03. Kabadivala — Traceable Recycling Platform

A role-based recycling platform connecting **customers, collectors, collection hubs, recyclers and administrators** in one traceable workflow — from pickup request to recycling completion and certificate generation.

**Key Features**
- 👤 Customer, Collector, Hub Manager, Recycler and Admin workspaces
- 📦 Pickup booking with scheduling, item details and images
- 📷 Camera-first and manual image upload workflow
- 🤖 Gemini-assisted item identification
- 🚚 Intelligent collector matching based on availability, capacity, category and service area
- ⚖️ Collection proof and actual-weight capture
- ⭐ Eco points, reviews, complaints and notifications
- 🏭 Hub inventory and recycling-batch management
- 🔗 QR handoffs and chain-of-custody tracking
- ♻️ Recycler processing stages and recovered-material records
- 📜 Recycling certificate generation
- 📊 Admin analytics, audit logs and operational management
- 📱 Responsive React interface with PWA support

**Tech Stack:** React, Vite, React Router, Node.js, Express.js, PostgreSQL, Prisma, JWT, Zod, Multer, Gemini Vision API, PDF/QR generation, Vercel, Render

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://kabadiwala-26.vercel.app/

🔗 **GitHub:** https://github.com/RajenderMohanVerma/Kabadiwala
